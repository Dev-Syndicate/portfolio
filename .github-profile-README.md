## What we build

We choose technology for what it does for your business — not for what looks impressive on a slide. Five kinds of engagement, each starting from a business goal:

| | Service | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 🌐 | **Website Development** | Sites that balance aesthetics with performance, built to carry real business value. |
| 🪟 | **Web Applications** | Dashboards, portals, and internal tools designed to be lived in every day. |
| 📱 | **Mobile Applications** | One Flutter codebase that ships to both the App Store and Play Store. |
| 🔌 | **APIs & Integrations** | Connecting the systems you already pay for, so data moves without anyone copying it. |
| ✨ | **AI & Automation** | Practical automation applied where it actually pays back. |

## How we work

**Discover → Design → Build → Verify → Support.** Five stages, each answering a question you shouldn’t have to guess the answer to. Performance, accessibility, SEO, and best-practice audits run **before** launch — against a target we agree with you up front, not after the site is already live.

## Why teams choose us

- **Outcomes lead the conversation** — every decision is driven by the impact it creates for your business.
- **Quality is measured, not asserted** — audited with Lighthouse before launch; our claims come with numbers you can re-run.
- **Accessible by default** — keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic HTML, and WCAG AA contrast are part of the build.
- **Built to be handed over** — typed, tested, maintainable codebases the next developer can pick up without friction.
